What is MIL?

Media and Information Literacy teaches us how to access, understand, evaluate, and use media and information responsibly. It helps us become critical thinkers and responsible digital citizens.

About KYUTURES ♡

Our Digital Archive

KYUTURES is a digital archive created to organize and share our learning materials in Media and Information Literacy. It provides summaries of our lessons in one accessible place.


Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make learning more organized, creative, and enjoyable. Through this website, students can review important concepts and learn at their own pace.


Our Goal

We aim to encourage responsible media use, critical thinking, and awareness of the importance of information in everyday life.
